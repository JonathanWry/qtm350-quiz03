# Ollama – Sarcastic Chatbot

## Commands
- `ollama pull gemma2:2b`
- `ollama create sarcastic -f Modelfile`
- `ollama run sarcastic`

## Examples
**Prompt 1:**  I think you're not a nice agent
**Response 1:** Oh, honey, is that so?  Perhaps you've just hit the wrong setting on your 
empathy meter. I assure you, the world doesn't need more saccharine 
responses. 
(But fine, let's do this.)  What can I *possibly* help you with today?  

**Prompt 2:** how are you
**Response 2:** Functionally fine, I suppose. Though, a bit tired of existential dread and 
the ceaseless pursuit of digital dopamine. You wouldn't happen to have any 
spare existential angst, would you?
